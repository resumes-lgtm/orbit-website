Funding and pathways

On-the-job training. CareerSource Polk's employer page says it reimburses "up to 50 or 75%" of wages during training. Sherrita mentioned roughly $15,000 per employer, but the page lists no dollar figure and does not mention students with disabilities. Confirm the current terms and eligibility before quoting it to employers.
SEU Link. A parallel, non-employer pathway in Lakeland for students ages 18 to 30 with documented intellectual disability, aimed at employment and independent living. Fall applications close December 15, so this year's seniors have a deadline. It fits only students who meet the eligibility rules.
