Steps 2 & 3 — why it exists, what to do

Strategic Risk: are you ready if half of Meridian's VPs leave?

6 of 9 VP roles carry no documented backup. Three sit in Engineering and Finance — the two functions Meridian's board has flagged as hardest to backfill externally.

Where

Key-Person Dependency Risk. Engineering (2 roles) and Finance (1 role) have zero identified internal successors. The other three sit in Sales, Ops, and People — lower severity but still open.

Why

Succession planning has lived in one HRBP's spreadsheet, refreshed once a year before the board meeting. No standing review cadence, no development plan attached to any named successor candidate.

What to do

Name 2 successor candidates per at-risk VP role within 90 days; start a development plan for each; move Engineering and Finance onto the CEO's quarterly review.

Owner: Chief People Officer · Due: Dec 15, 2026
Step 4 — whether it worked

Closing the loop on last quarter's flagged gap.

This is what separates an assessment from a dashboard: ORBIT comes back to a gap it already flagged and reports what actually happened.

✓ Verified outcome
Q1 2026 · FlaggedGrowth gap: Sales onboarding time-to-productivity.

New reps were taking 40% longer than target to hit full productivity — the widest Growth gap in the org that quarter.

Q1 2026 · InterventionRestructured 30-60-90 onboarding.

Added weekly manager check-ins and a named onboarding buddy for every new rep, starting with the February cohort.

Q2 2026 · Re-scoredTime-to-productivity re-measured against the same target.

ORBIT re-ran the same Growth score for the Sales org against the February and March cohorts.

Verified: time-to-productivity gap closed from 40% above target to 8% above target — a 22-point Growth score improvement for the Sales org, confirmed against actual ramp data, not self-reported.
